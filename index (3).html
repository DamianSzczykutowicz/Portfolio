02 About

I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at the Rutgers Honors College, with a minor in Computer Science, drawn to the point where hardware meets software: designing structures, then bringing them to life with embedded systems and control.

My work spans CAD modeling and FEA simulation in SolidWorks, data-driven analysis in MATLAB and Python, and embedded integration with platforms like Arduino, STM32, and the Jetson Nano. My Novartis internship added a manufacturing side: equipment qualification, GxP documentation, and reliability analysis in a regulated production environment.

When I'm not in the shop or the lab, I'm usually tinkering with a side project or refining the next iteration of a part.
